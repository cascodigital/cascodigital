## BananaPhone

<a href="https://github.com/cascodigital/bananaphone"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cascodigital/bananaphone/main/docs/screenshots/dictate-coach.png" alt="BananaPhone dictating with the language coach" width="640"></a>

**[BananaPhone](https://github.com/cascodigital/bananaphone)** turns dictation into professional text on your clipboard, in Portuguese, Spanish or English, and tells you what you got wrong in the language you are still learning. It also creates Jira tickets in one click. Runs on cloud models (OpenAI, Gemini) or 100% offline with Ollama and Whisper. Windows and Linux.
